## 7. Why R1 can still complete

R1 is the **Editorial Foundation** program.

Its job is to make identity, knowledge, Evidence, governance, language, route, document, surface, distribution and compatibility semantics executable and mutually coherent before presentation takes authority.

That job is complete when a future renderer can consume one deterministic accepted state without needing to answer new semantic questions.

The next program may then materialize the publication shell and physical cutover as implementation over this accepted substrate.
