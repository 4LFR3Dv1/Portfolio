## Constitutional principles

### EC-01 — Public truth outranks presentation
A public interface MUST NOT strengthen, broaden or upgrade a claim beyond the evidence and scope available to the underlying record.

### EC-02 — Publication is not the source of truth
A publication MAY interpret records, but it MUST NOT become the canonical authority for facts that belong to systems, experiments, claims or evidence.

### EC-03 — Evidence is not a claim
An artifact, commit, test, deployment, endpoint, document, witness or dataset MUST NOT determine its own conclusion merely by existing.

### EC-04 — Claim is not evidence
A claim MUST NOT be treated as self-validating. Claims that require support MUST be able to reference the evidence, observations or prior claims that support them.

### EC-05 — Questions may remain open
An unanswered question is a valid public knowledge state. The system MUST permit questions to remain open, partially answered or superseded without manufacturing closure.

### EC-06 — Unknown remains unknown
Absence of evidence MUST NOT be converted into success, failure, safety, correctness or any other conclusion by inference alone.

### EC-07 — System is not publication
A durable system identity MUST exist independently of any article, essay, case study or research note written about it.

### EC-08 — Experiment is not narrative
An experiment and the editorial account of that experiment MUST be independently identifiable. Editing a narrative MUST NOT silently rewrite the experimental result.

### EC-09 — Visibility is multidimensional
Public visibility, source availability, evidence availability, maturity and disclosure MUST NOT be collapsed into one field or one label.

### EC-10 — Architecture is representation
Architecture records describe responsibilities, boundaries, relationships or invariants. They MUST NOT be treated as proof that the represented system behaves as described.

### EC-11 — Published URLs are durable identities
Once a public canonical URL is established, a later migration MUST preserve its identity through continued resolution or an explicit durable redirect. Content MUST NOT silently move to a different identity.

### EC-12 — Revision preserves history
A material correction or constitutional change MUST remain distinguishable from the state that preceded it. Frozen contracts MUST be amended explicitly rather than silently rewritten.

### EC-13 — Private knowledge cannot leak by composition
A set of individually public records MUST NOT be linked or projected in a way that reconstructs information deliberately withheld by a private or sanitized boundary.

### EC-14 — UI is not ontology
Cards, sections, pages, tabs, navigation, search results and visual templates are replaceable projections. They MUST NOT define what the underlying records are.

### EC-15 — Code is not evidence of operation by itself
Public source proves that source is public. It MUST NOT, by itself, be presented as proof of deployment, runtime behavior, production maturity, security review or successful physical execution.

## Priority rule
When future choices conflict, preserve public truth, explicit uncertainty, provenance and disclosure boundaries before convenience, aesthetics or marketing value.

## Amendment rule
Any material change to EC-01 through EC-15 MUST explicitly record affected IDs, previous normative text, replacement normative text, reason for change and migration impact. Historical constitutional text MUST remain reconstructable.

## Scope
The constitution applies to records, claims, evidence, provenance, system and experiment identity, architecture, publications, route/language identity, derived views and disclosure boundaries. It does not choose framework, file format, design system or deployment provider.
